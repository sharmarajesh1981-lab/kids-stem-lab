🧪 SCIENCE⏱ ABOUT 10 MIN⭐ BEGINNER

Rainbow
in a Jar

Can you stack a rainbow? Make colorful layers and discover why some liquids float on top of others.

COLORFULHANDS-ONGROWN-UP HELPER
✳✦
FIRST, GATHER YOUR GEAR

What you’ll need

  • 🥛 A tall, clear glass or jar
  • 💧 Water
  • 🍯 Honey or corn syrup
  • 🧼 Dish soap
  • 🫒 Cooking oil
  • 🥄 A spoon and a few small cups
  • 🎨 Food coloring (optional)
ONE STEP AT A TIME

Let’s make it!

  1. 01

    Make your colors

    Pour a little water, dish soap, and honey into separate cups. Add a different food color to each one if you like.

  2. 02

    Start with the heaviest

    Ask a grown-up to help. Slowly pour the honey into the bottom of your clear jar.

  3. 03

    Pour like a scientist

    Tip each liquid gently over the back of a spoon. Try not to splash or mix the layers.

  4. 04

    Add the oil and observe

    Pour the oil last. What do you notice? Draw your rainbow or tell someone what you discovered.

🤔
Wonder why?

Different liquids have different densities—some are more “packed together” than others. Denser liquids sink below less-dense liquids. Can you guess which layer is densest?
