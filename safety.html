A NOTE FOR KIDS & GROWN-UPS

Curious is cool.
Safe is super.

The best discoveries happen when everyone feels comfortable, prepared, and ready to have fun.

🦺
Make it a team project.

Ask a trusted grown-up to read the instructions and stay nearby while you experiment.

🙋01 / ASK FIRST

Bring your grown-up in.

Check with an adult before starting. They can help choose a safe place, gather supplies, and handle tricky steps.

🧤02 / GET READY

Protect your workspace.

Use a clear, stable table. Wear old clothes or an apron, and follow any project-specific gear instructions.

🚫03 / KEEP IT OUT

Experiments aren’t snacks.

Never eat or drink project materials. Keep supplies away from your face, and wash your hands when you finish.

🧹04 / WRAP IT UP

Clean together.

Ask an adult how to dispose of materials. Wipe down your workspace and put supplies away safely.

✳

Every project is different.

These are general reminders, not a substitute for adult supervision. Read each project’s supplies and instructions together, and skip anything that doesn’t feel safe for your family.
